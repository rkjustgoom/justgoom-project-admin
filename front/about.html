About Just Goom LLP

Building trusted partnerships through innovation — connecting people with the services they need.

Partnership & Innovation at Our Core

Just Goom LLP is a dynamic local services platform built on the foundation of trust, partnership, and technology. Our logo — featuring interlocking hands and circuit patterns — represents our commitment to connecting people with reliable services through innovative digital solutions.

From real estate and entertainment to health tools and expert blogs, we curate the best local listings and resources to help millions of users discover everything they need in one trusted place.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, we serve communities across India with a mission to simplify local discovery and empower businesses to reach their audience effectively.
